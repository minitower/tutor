# Facilitator Script — Session 6: Show Someone

Read the spoken lines out loud to the kid. **[Grown-Up action]** lines
are things you do, not say. **[Grown-Up note]** lines are for you only —
don't read them out loud.

---

## Slide 1 — Look what you've built!

"Think about everything you've made so far — your name page, your
button, your colors, your story, your bug fix. That's a lot!"

**[Grown-Up action]** If you can, quickly pull up a couple of the past
builds so the kid sees them again for a second.

---

## Slide 2 — Today is different

"Today we're not building anything new. Today's job is to show one of
your things to somebody else."

---

## Slide 3 — Pick your favorite

"Which one is your favorite? Pick whichever one you're most proud of."

**[Grown-Up action]** Open that build so it's ready to hand over.

---

## Slide 4 — Who's going to try it?

"Now let's find someone who has never seen this before — Mom, Dad, your
brother or sister, a friend, even a grandparent on a video call."

**[Grown-Up action]** Help set up that person, in person or on a call.

---

## Slide 5 — Hand it over and watch

"Give it to them and just say 'try this.' Don't explain how it works.
Then watch what they do."

**[Grown-Up note]** This is the important part for you too: stay quiet
and watch, just like the kid. Resist the urge to jump in and help the
other person — even if it's tempting. Watching someone struggle a
little, unaided, is the whole point of today.

---

## Slide 6 — What did they do?

"What did they click first? Did they do what you expected? Did anything
confuse them?"

Wait for the kid to answer before saying anything else.

---

## Slide 7 — You noticed something

"That's a real thing that people who build stuff for a living do too —
someone who didn't make it notices things the builder stopped seeing.
Was there anything they didn't get that felt obvious to you?"

---

## Slide 8 — Nice work today!

"You built something, and today you watched someone else use it. That's
a big deal. Great job!"
