# Session 9 — Show Someone

## For You

Today there's no new idea to write. Instead, pick your favorite thing
you've made so far and show it to someone who's never seen it before —
and watch what they do, without helping them.

## Idea Card

None today — pick one thing you already built (Session 0–8) to show.

## For the Grown-Up

1. Have the kid pick their favorite build from earlier sessions.
2. Find someone who hasn't seen it (a sibling, a grandparent on a video
   call, a friend) and have the kid hand it over with as little
   explanation as possible — just "try this."
3. Watch quietly. Don't jump in to explain or help unless the person is
   truly stuck.
4. Afterward, ask the kid what they noticed: did the other person do
   what was expected? Get confused anywhere? Click something the kid
   didn't think they would?

## Show & Tell

Ask the kid: "Was there anything they didn't understand that felt
obvious to you?" This is the same idea grown-up software teams use —
someone who didn't build a thing notices what the builder can't see
anymore because they're too close to it.
