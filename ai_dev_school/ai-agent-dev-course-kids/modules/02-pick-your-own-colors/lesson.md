# Session 2 — Pick Your Own Colors

## For You

Right now your page's colors were picked for you. Today *you* pick them
— so it looks like something you made, not something that just happened.

## Idea Card

> The background is ______.
> The button is ______.
> The words are ______.

(Use color names the kid actually knows — sky blue, grass green, sunset
orange — not codes. That's the grown-up's job to translate.)

## For the Grown-Up

1. Fill out the card. If helpful, have a few labeled color swatches on
   paper or screen to point at instead of naming colors from memory.
2. Instruction to Claude Code: *"Update the page's colors: background
   [color], button [color], text [color]. Keep everything else the
   same."*
3. Approve, narrate, open the result.
4. This is a good moment to build a page from Session 0 or 1 a second
   time later and compare — same idea, different colors, still clearly
   "theirs" both times because *they* picked the colors on purpose.

## Show & Tell

Ask: "If your best friend made their own page, what colors do you think
they'd pick, and how would that make it look different from yours?"
