# Session 6 — Say Exactly What You Mean

## For You

The machine can only do what you actually tell it — it can't read your
mind. Today you'll ask for the same thing two ways: a loose, fuzzy way,
and a clear, exact way — and see how much closer the exact one gets to
what you really wanted.

## Idea Card

> Fuzzy: "I want a fun ______."
> Exact: "I want a ______ where [who/what] ______ [does what] to
> [who/what] ______."

Example: Fuzzy — "I want a fun game." Exact — "I want a game where a cat
catches three falling stars."

## For the Grown-Up

1. Do the fuzzy version first: give Claude Code exactly the fuzzy
   sentence, nothing more. Show the kid what comes back.
2. Then do the exact version as its own fresh build (don't just edit the
   first one — a new attempt makes the comparison clean).
3. Put both side by side (two browser tabs is enough).

## Show & Tell

Ask: "Which one is closer to what was actually in your head? What did
you say in the exact one that you didn't say in the fuzzy one?" That
last question is the whole lesson — let the kid find the answer instead
of telling them.
