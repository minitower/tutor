# Facilitator Script — Session 3: Say Exactly What You Mean

Read the spoken lines out loud to the kid. **[Grown-Up action]** lines
are things you do, not say.

---

## Slide 1 — Welcome back!

"Hey! Last time you picked all the colors for your page. Today we're
going to learn something that makes everything you build from now on
better."

---

## Slide 2 — Today: say exactly what you mean

"The machine can only do what you actually tell it. It can't guess
what's in your head — it can only read your words. Today we're going to
test that out."

---

## Slide 3 — Fuzzy vs. exact

"There are two ways to ask for something: a fuzzy way, where you just
say a little bit, and an exact way, where you say exactly what you
want, with details. We're going to try both — for the exact same idea —
and see what happens."

---

## Slide 4 — Your Idea Card

"First, the fuzzy way: I want a fun... what? Just pick one word."

**[Grown-Up action]** Write down the kid's fuzzy word (for example,
"game").

"Now the exact way. Same kind of thing, but this time tell me who does
what to who. Like: 'I want a game where a cat catches three falling
stars.' What's yours?"

**[Grown-Up action]** Write down the kid's exact sentence, filling in
all the blanks. Help with wording if they get stuck — the example on
the card is there to help.

---

## Slide 5 — Watch the fuzzy idea build

"First, let's give the machine only the fuzzy sentence — nothing else."

**[Grown-Up action]** Open Claude Code and type in exactly the fuzzy
sentence, nothing more (e.g., "I want a fun game."). Read it out loud
before sending it. Approve its actions as it works.

---

## Slide 6 — Look what the fuzzy idea made

"Let's see what it made!"

**[Grown-Up action]** Open the result in a browser tab. Let the kid
look and react before you say anything.

---

## Slide 7 — Now watch the exact idea build

"Now let's try the exact sentence — as a brand new build, not by
changing the first one."

**[Grown-Up action]** Start a fresh build in Claude Code (a new
file/folder, not an edit to the fuzzy one) and type in the kid's exact
sentence. Read it out loud before sending it. Approve its actions as it
works.

---

## Slide 8 — Look what the exact idea made

"Let's see this one too!"

**[Grown-Up action]** Open this result in a second browser tab, next to
the first one.

---

## Slide 9 — Which one is closer?

"Look at both of them side by side. Which one is closer to what was
actually in your head? What did you say in the exact one that you
didn't say in the fuzzy one?"

Let the kid answer both questions in their own words — don't tell them,
let them find it.

---

## Slide 10 — You did it!

"You asked for the same idea two ways, and saw how much closer the
exact one got. That's the whole secret to getting the machine to build
exactly what you want. Nice work today!"
