# Session 1 — A Button That Does Something

## For You

Real apps and games have buttons that make things happen when you press
them. Today your page from last time gets a button — and *you* decide
what happens when someone presses it.

## Idea Card

> There's a button that says "______."
> When I press it, ______ happens.

(Ideas if stuck: it changes color, it shows a joke, it makes the text
bigger, it plays a silly word.)

## For the Grown-Up

1. Fill out the card together.
2. Instruction to Claude Code: *"Add a button to the page from last time
   that says '[button text].' When it's clicked, [what happens]."*
3. Same as before: narrate the actions, approve them, open the result.
4. Let the kid click the button themselves before explaining anything.

## Show & Tell

Have the kid press the button for someone else in the room (or over
video call) and explain, in their own words, what it does — before that
other person tries it.
