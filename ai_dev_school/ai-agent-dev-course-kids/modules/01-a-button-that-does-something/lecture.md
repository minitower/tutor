# Facilitator Script — Session 1: A Button That Does Something

Read the spoken lines out loud to the kid. **[Grown-Up action]** lines
are things you do, not say.

---

## Slide 1 — Welcome back!

"Hey! Remember last time, when we made your page with your name on it?
Today we're going to make it even better."

**[Grown-Up action]** Open the page from Session 0 so the kid can see it
again for a second before moving on.

---

## Slide 2 — Today: buttons!

"Today's big idea is buttons. A button is something you press, and
something happens because you pressed it. Real games and apps are full
of them."

---

## Slide 3 — Your Idea Card

"Let's fill out your Idea Card. There's a button that says... what?
What do you want it to say?"

**[Grown-Up action]** Write down the kid's answer for the button text.
Help with spelling if needed.

"Okay, and when you press it, what should happen?"

**[Grown-Up action]** Write down the kid's answer for what happens.

---

## Slide 4 — Stuck? Try one of these

"If you're not sure, here are some ideas: the button could change the
color, show a joke, make the words bigger, or say something silly. Pick
whichever one sounds the most fun to you."

---

## Slide 5 — Watch the machine work

"Now I'm going to tell the machine exactly what you said."

**[Grown-Up action]** Open Claude Code and type an instruction built
from the card, for example: *"Add a button to the page from last time
that says '[button text].' When it's clicked, [what happens]."* Read the
instruction out loud before sending it. As Claude Code works, narrate
each action out loud and approve it — e.g., "it wants to change the
page, is that okay?"

---

## Slide 6 — Press the button!

"It's ready! Go ahead — open it up and press your button."

**[Grown-Up action]** Open the result in the browser. Let the kid be the
one to click it. Don't explain anything yet — just watch.

---

## Slide 7 — What do you think?

"Is that what you imagined when you thought of it? Tell me what
happened!"

Wait for the kid's reaction before saying anything else.

---

## Slide 8 — Show someone!

"Now find someone else — Mom, Dad, your sister, anyone nearby — and
press the button for them. But explain what it's going to do *first*,
before you press it."

**[Grown-Up action]** Help find that person or set up a quick video call
if no one else is around right now.

---

## Slide 9 — You did it!

"You told the machine exactly what you wanted, and it did it. That's a
button that does something — and you made it. Nice work!"
