# Session 0 — Meet the Idea Machine

## For You

There's a machine that can read words you write and turn them into a
real thing you can see on a screen — like magic, except it's not magic,
it's just really good at reading exactly what you tell it. Today you're
going to write down one tiny idea, and watch it become real.

## Idea Card

> My name is ______.
> My favorite ______ is ______.

(Fill in the second blank with a category — animal, color, food,
game — and the third with your answer.)

## For the Grown-Up

1. Fill out the Idea Card together — read it out loud, let the kid say
   the answers, help with spelling if needed.
2. Turn it into a plain instruction for Claude Code, e.g.: *"Make a
   simple, colorful local web page that says hello to [name] and shows
   that their favorite [category] is [answer]. Keep it simple — just
   text, maybe an emoji or two."*
3. Approve its actions as it works, narrating what it's doing ("it's
   making a new file now").
4. Open the result in a browser and let the kid see it before you say
   anything about it.

## Show & Tell

Ask: "Is that what you pictured when you told it your idea?" That's the
whole checkpoint — no writing required.
