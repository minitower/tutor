# Facilitator Script — Session 0: Meet the Idea Machine

Read the lines out loud. Go at the kid's pace — this whole session
should take 15–25 minutes. **[Grown-Up action]** lines are things you
do, not things you say.

## Slide 1 — The Idea Machine

"Hi! Today we're going to meet something really cool. It's called the
Idea Machine."

"It can take words — just words you write — and turn them into a real
thing you can see on the screen."

## Slide 2 — What is the Idea Machine?

"Here's the trick: you tell it your idea in plain words, and it reads
exactly what you said, and builds it. It's not magic — it's just really
good at reading and building."

## Slide 3 — What we're making today

"Today we're keeping it super simple. We're going to make a page that
has two things on it: your name, and your favorite thing."

## Slide 4 — Your Idea Card

"Let's fill out your Idea Card together."

"What's your name?"

"And what's your favorite thing? First, tell me the category — is it an
animal, a color, a food, a game, something else? ... Okay, now what's
your favorite one?"

**[Grown-Up action]** Help the kid fill in all three blanks on the Idea
Card. Help with spelling if needed. Read the finished card back to them.

## Slide 5 — Watch the machine work

"Now I'm going to tell the machine your idea, and we're going to watch
it build."

**[Grown-Up action]** Open Claude Code and type an instruction based on
the filled-in Idea Card, for example: *"Make a simple, colorful local
web page that says hello to [name] and shows that their favorite
[category] is [answer]. Keep it simple — just text, maybe an emoji or
two."* Approve its actions as it works, narrating out loud what's
happening — "it's making a new file now," "it's picking colors now."

## Slide 6 — Ta-da!

**[Grown-Up action]** Open the finished page in a browser. Let the kid
look at it for a moment before you say anything about it.

"Ta-da! There it is — that's yours."

## Slide 7 — What do you think?

"Is that what you pictured when you told it your idea?"

That's the whole checkpoint — no writing, no right answer. Just listen
to what they say.

## Slide 8 — You did it!

"You just told a machine an idea, and it became a real thing you can
click on. That's what this whole class is about."

"Nice work today!"
