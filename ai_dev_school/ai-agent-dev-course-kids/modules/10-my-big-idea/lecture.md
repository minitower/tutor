# Facilitator Script — Session 10: My Big Idea

Read the spoken lines out loud to the kid. **[Grown-Up action]** lines
are things you do, not say. This is the last session — let it feel like
a celebration, and let it run a bit longer than usual (20–30 minutes is
fine) since there's a full Idea Card to fill out this time.

---

## Slide 1 — This Is It — Your Final Session!

"This is our very last session together — and it's your biggest one
yet."

**[Grown-Up action]** If you can, take a beat before starting to think
about (and maybe say out loud) how far things have come since Session
0.

---

## Slide 2 — Everything You've Learned

"Think about everything you've made: your name page, a button, your own
colors, saying exactly what you mean, a story with choices, finding a
bug, and showing someone your work. Today, all of that goes into one
big idea — yours."

---

## Slide 3 — Today: It's All Yours

"Today I'm not going to give you any hints about what to build. This
one is completely up to you."

---

## Slide 4 — Your Big Idea Card

"This is the biggest Idea Card yet — it's really four cards in one. It
asks what your idea is called, what happens when it starts, what
happens when you press or choose something, and what colors it uses."

**[Grown-Up action]** Show the kid the full card and read each line out
loud, slowly.

---

## Slide 5 — Need a Spark?

"If you're not sure yet, think about something you'd actually want to
play with — a game, a silly page, a creature that talks back. What
sounds fun to you?"

---

## Slide 6 — Let's Fill It Out — Together

"Let's go blank by blank. Say each answer out loud first, and then
we'll write it down."

**[Grown-Up action]** Work through the card with the kid, one line at a
time. Help with spelling. If an answer is vague, ask a narrowing
question ("what happens *first* when someone opens it?") instead of
steering them toward something easier to build.

---

## Slide 7 — First: What Happens When It Starts?

"What's the very first thing someone sees when they open your idea?"

**[Grown-Up action]** Write down their answer on the card.

---

## Slide 8 — Next: What Happens When You Press or Choose?

"And what happens when someone presses a button or picks something —
like your button from Session 4, or your story choices from Session
7?"

**[Grown-Up action]** Write down their answer.

---

## Slide 9 — Now: Pick Your Colors

"Last part — what colors does it use? Background, buttons, words, all
of it."

**[Grown-Up action]** Write down their color choices, same as Session
5.

---

## Slide 10 — Watch It Come to Life

"Now I'm going to turn your whole card into one instruction for the
machine."

**[Grown-Up action]** Open Claude Code and type a single instruction
that combines everything on the card — what it's called, what happens
at the start, what the buttons/choices do, and the colors. Read the
instruction out loud before sending it. Narrate and approve each action
as it works, the same way you have all session.

---

## Slide 11 — The Big Reveal

"It's ready. Let's open it up and see your idea, for real."

**[Grown-Up action]** Open the result. Let the kid look and interact
with it first, before saying anything.

---

## Slide 12 — What Do You Think?

"Is that what you imagined? Tell me what's the same, and what's
different."

Wait for their reaction before saying anything else. If something's
off, this is a real chance to use the Bug Hunt skill from Session 8 —
let them say what's wrong before you fix it together.

---

## Slide 13 — Show It Off

"Now let's do what we practiced in Session 9 — find someone and show
them your idea."

**[Grown-Up action]** Help find that person, in person or on a call.
Afterward, ask the kid to explain, in their own words, what they asked
for and what the machine actually built.

---

## Slide 14 — You're an Idea Machine Builder!

"You had an idea, you wrote it down, and the machine built exactly what
you said. That's the whole magic trick — and now you know how it works.
You did every bit of this yourself. Amazing work, all the way through."
