# Session 10 — My Big Idea

## For You

This is your idea, your way, start to finish. Think of something you'd
actually want to make — not what you think you're supposed to make —
and let's build it.

## Idea Card

> What it's called: ______
> What happens when it starts: ______
> What happens when you press/choose ______: ______
> The colors are: ______

(This combines everything from Sessions 0–7 into one card. Take your
time on it — talk it through out loud before writing.)

## For the Grown-Up

1. Let the idea be genuinely theirs, even if it's ambitious or a little
   unclear at first — help them narrow it with questions ("what happens
   *first* when someone opens it?") rather than steering it toward
   something easier to build.
2. Turn the filled-out card into a clear instruction for Claude Code,
   combining what it does, what the buttons/choices do, and the colors,
   the same way you have all along.
3. Approve, narrate, open the result.
4. If it's not quite right, this is a real chance to use Session 8's
   Bug Hunt skill for real — let the kid say what's off before you fix
   it together.

## Show & Tell

Show it to family or friends, the way Session 9 practiced. Ask the kid
to explain, in their own words, what they asked for and what the
machine actually built — that gap (or lack of one) is the whole course,
summed up in one sentence they get to say themselves.
