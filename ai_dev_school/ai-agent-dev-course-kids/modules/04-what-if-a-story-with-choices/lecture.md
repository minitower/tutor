# Session 4 — Facilitator Script: What If...? A Story With Choices

Read the spoken lines out loud to the kid. **[Grown-Up action]** lines
are things you do, not say. Target time: 15–25 minutes.

## Slide 1 — Welcome back!

"Hey! Remember last time, we found out the machine only does exactly
what we tell it — no more, no less?"

"Today we're doing something different. Today we're making a **story**."

## Slide 2 — Today's Big Idea

"Most stories only go one way, right? You read page one, then page two,
then page three."

"But today, YOUR story is going to go **two** ways. The person reading
it gets to pick what happens."

## Slide 3 — A Story Can Fork!

"Look at this — say you find a mystery door in your backyard. If you
**open it**, maybe there's a room full of bouncy balls! But if you
**knock first**, maybe a tiny dragon answers and says hello!"

"Same door. Same story. Two totally different things could happen —
just because of what you choose."

**[Grown-Up action]** Point at the arrows on the slide as you read each
branch, so the fork is visual, not just spoken.

## Slide 4 — What We're Making Today

"That's what we're building. A tiny story with a beginning, two
choices, and a different ending for each one."

## Slide 5 — Your Idea Card

"Let's figure out your story! First let's just talk about it — don't
write anything yet."

"How does your story start? ... Okay! Now, what are the two things
someone reading it could choose? ... And what happens for each one?"

**[Grown-Up action]** Talk it through out loud first. Once it feels
solid, help the kid write it onto the Idea Card:

> The story starts: ______.
> If you choose ______, then ______ happens.
> If you choose ______, then ______ happens.

## Slide 6 — Watch the Machine Build It

"Now watch — I'm going to tell the machine your story, word for word."

**[Grown-Up action]** Type into Claude Code: *"Make a simple local web
page that tells this short story: [story start]. Then show two buttons:
'[choice 1]' and '[choice 2].' Clicking each one shows its outcome:
[outcome 1] / [outcome 2]."* Narrate what you're typing and approve the
actions out loud as they come up. Open the result.

## Slide 7 — Try Choice A!

"Go ahead — click the first button!"

**[Grown-Up action]** Let the kid click it themselves. Wait for their
reaction before saying anything.

"Is that what you pictured happening?"

## Slide 8 — Try Choice B!

"Now let's go back and try the OTHER one."

**[Grown-Up action]** Let the kid click the second button.

"See that? Same story, but a totally different ending — just because you
picked something else."

## Slide 9 — What Do You Think?

"Which ending did you like better?"

"Did the machine do exactly what we wrote on the card?"

## Slide 10 — You built a story with choices!

"You just made a story that can go two different ways — and both of
those ways came from your own head. That's awesome work today!"

**[Grown-Up action]** Before wrapping up, set up Show & Tell: have the
kid pick one branch to show someone else (another family member, or on
a call), and have that person guess what the *other* choice would have
done before actually showing them.
