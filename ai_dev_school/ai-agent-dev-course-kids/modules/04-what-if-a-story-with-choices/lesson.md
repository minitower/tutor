# Session 4 — What If...? A Story With Choices

## For You

Stories don't have to go just one way. Today you'll write a tiny story
where the person reading it gets to pick what happens next — like a
choose-your-own-adventure book, but one you made.

## Idea Card

> The story starts: ______.
> If you choose ______, then ______ happens.
> If you choose ______, then ______ happens.

(Keep it to two choices and a short outcome for each — this isn't meant
to be a whole book.)

## For the Grown-Up

1. Fill out the card together — this one benefits from talking it
   through out loud before writing anything down.
2. Instruction to Claude Code: *"Make a simple local web page that tells
   this short story: [story start]. Then show two buttons: '[choice 1]'
   and '[choice 2].' Clicking each one shows its outcome: [outcome 1] /
   [outcome 2]."*
3. Approve, narrate, open the result — then actually click through both
   choices together.

## Show & Tell

Let the kid pick one of the two branches to show someone else, and ask
that person to guess what the *other* choice might have done before
showing them.
