# Facilitator Script — Session 5: Pick Your Own Colors

Read the spoken lines out loud to the kid. **[Grown-Up action]** lines
are things you do, not say.

---

## Slide 1 — Welcome back!

"Hey! Remember your page from before, with your button on it? Let's
look at it for a second."

**[Grown-Up action]** Open the page from Session 0 or 4 so the kid sees
it again for a moment before moving on.

---

## Slide 2 — Today: pick your own colors!

"Right now, the colors on your page were just picked for you — nobody
asked you. Today, *you* get to pick them, on purpose. That's the whole
idea."

---

## Slide 3 — Meet the colors

"Here are some colors to choose from: sky blue, grass green, and sunset
orange."

**[Grown-Up action]** If you have paper or on-screen color swatches,
show them now and point at each one as you say its name. If not, just
say the names clearly.

---

## Slide 4 — Your Idea Card

"Let's fill out your Idea Card. What color should the background be?"

**[Grown-Up action]** Write down the kid's answer for the background.

"What color should the button be?"

**[Grown-Up action]** Write down the kid's answer for the button.

"And what color should the words be?"

**[Grown-Up action]** Write down the kid's answer for the words. Read
the whole finished card back to them.

---

## Slide 5 — Watch the machine work

"Now I'm going to tell the machine exactly which colors you picked."

**[Grown-Up action]** Open Claude Code and type an instruction built
from the card, for example: *"Update the page's colors: background
[color], button [color], text [color]. Keep everything else the same."*
Read the instruction out loud before sending it. As Claude Code works,
narrate each action out loud and approve it.

---

## Slide 6 — Ta-da!

**[Grown-Up action]** Open the result in the browser. Let the kid look
at it for a moment before you say anything.

"Ta-da! There it is — in the colors you picked."

---

## Slide 7 — What do you think?

"Does it feel like your page now? Tell me what you think!"

Wait for the kid's reaction before saying anything else.

---

## Slide 8 — What about a friend?

"If your best friend made their own page, what colors do you think
they'd pick? How would that make their page look different from
yours?"

This is just a conversation — no writing, no right answer.

---

## Slide 9 — You did it!

"You picked your colors on purpose, instead of just letting them
happen. That's what makes this page really, truly yours. Nice work
today!"

**[Grown-Up action]** If there's time, this is a great moment to build a
page from Session 0 or 4 again later with different colors, and compare
the two.
