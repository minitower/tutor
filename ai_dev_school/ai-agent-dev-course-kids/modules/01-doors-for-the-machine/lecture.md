# Facilitator Script — Session 1: Doors for the Machine

Read the spoken lines out loud to the kid. **[Grown-Up action]** lines
are things you do, not say.

---

## Slide 1 — Welcome back!

"Hi! You've already made a page with your name on it. Today we're giving the machine a real superpower."

---

## Slide 2 — Today: Doors for the Machine!

"Picture this: the machine lives in a room with no windows. It sees only what's inside. Can it find out what the weather is today?" Let the kid answer.

---

## Slide 3 — A door is a way through

"And if we make a door in the room — with just one list behind it? Then the machine can read it. Which door to open is up to you."

---

## Slide 4 — Your Idea Card

"Here's the card. Today we'll fill it in together."

**[Grown-Up action]** Read the card aloud, but don't fill it in yet.

---

## Slide 5 — Look-only doors

"Some doors you can only look through — like a shop window. We always open those: looking is fine, breaking is not."

---

## Slide 6 — Let's make the list

"Name three animals you like, and one fact about each." Write the answers down.

**[Grown-Up action]** Create the `facts` folder and `animals.txt`; type what the kid says.

---

## Slide 7 — Opening the door

"Now I'll open a door — only to this list. See, I'm not opening anything else."

**[Grown-Up action]** Connect the Filesystem server to the `facts` folder and deny the write tools. Read aloud what Claude Code asks.

---

## Slide 8 — Ask through the door

"Let's ask the machine: which animal on your list is the biggest?" Wait for the answer together.

**[Grown-Up action]** Send the question. Let the kid react first.

---

## Slide 9 — What if the door is closed?

"I'll close the door — and ask the same thing. What do you think will happen?" Let them guess. "See? Without the door the machine can't see."

**[Grown-Up action]** Remove the server or switch it off in `/mcp`.

---

## Slide 10 — You're a door keeper!

"What door did we open? What could the machine do through it — and what couldn't it?" Let them answer in their own words. "Today you were a door keeper."
