# Facilitator Script — Session 2: The Notebook That Remembers

Read the spoken lines out loud to the kid. **[Grown-Up action]** lines
are things you do, not say.

---

## Slide 1 — Welcome back!

"Hi! Last time we opened doors for the machine. Today we'll talk about something the machine forgets."

---

## Slide 2 — Today: The Notebook That Remembers

"When I close the machine and open it again, it doesn't remember anything from yesterday. Like a goldfish! Every morning is a blank page."

---

## Slide 3 — A notebook helps

"But if it has a notebook that it reads every morning — it will remember everything written there."

---

## Slide 4 — Your Idea Card

"Here's the card. First, favorites. And at the bottom — what we never write."

**[Grown-Up action]** Read the card aloud.

---

## Slide 5 — Secrets stay secret

"Some things are secrets: where you live, your school's name, passwords. We don't write those in the notebook. The notebook is only for fun favorites." Ask: "Why do you think that is?"

**[Grown-Up action]** Let the kid explain first. If needed: "Machines and other people at the computer can read the notebook."

---

## Slide 6 — Writing in the notebook

"What's your favorite color? What do you love?" Write it word for word.

**[Grown-Up action]** Create/open `CLAUDE.md` in the course folder and put in the answers.

---

## Slide 7 — A new day!

"Now it's a new day — the machine just woke up and remembers nothing. I won't tell it any color or animal. Only: 'Make a page I'll like.'"

**[Grown-Up action]** Close Claude Code, reopen it, send the request.

---

## Slide 8 — It remembers!

Wait for the kid's reaction. "How did it know you like green?" Let them guess: "It read the notebook!"

---

## Slide 9 — Forgetting on purpose

"Now let's erase the kittens line. What will happen?" Erase, restart, ask for the same page. "It forgot! You're the notebook boss."

**[Grown-Up action]** Delete a line from `CLAUDE.md`.

---

## Slide 10 — You're the notebook boss!

"What did we write? And what do we never write — and why?" Let them answer. "Today you were a notebook keeper."
