# Session 8 — Bug Hunt: Find What's Silly

## For You

Sometimes when you ask for something, the machine gets a little bit of
it wrong or silly — and that's completely normal, even for grown-up
programmers! Today you're going to find something that's wrong on
purpose, and be the one to fix it by explaining what *should* happen
instead.

## Idea Card

> I asked for: ______ (pick something from an earlier session)
> What's silly or wrong about it: ______
> What should happen instead: ______

## For the Grown-Up

1. Before the session, quietly ask Claude Code to rebuild one of the
   earlier ideas with one small thing deliberately off — the wrong
   color, a button that does the wrong thing, a typo in the text. Don't
   tell the kid what it is.
2. Let the kid look at (and click around in) the result and try to
   find what's off, before you point at anything.
3. Once they've found it (help if they get stuck — this isn't a test),
   fill out the rest of the card: what's wrong, what it should be
   instead.
4. Send that as the fix instruction to Claude Code and watch it get
   corrected.

## Show & Tell

Ask: "How did you know something was wrong?" Getting the kid to
articulate *how they noticed* is more valuable than the fix itself —
that noticing is the actual skill.
