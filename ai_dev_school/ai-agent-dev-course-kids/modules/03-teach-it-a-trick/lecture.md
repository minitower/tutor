# Facilitator Script — Session 3: Teach the Machine a Trick

Read the spoken lines out loud to the kid. **[Grown-Up action]** lines
are things you do, not say.

---

## Slide 1 — Welcome back!

"Hi! You can already open doors and keep a notebook. Today we'll teach the machine a real trick."

---

## Slide 2 — Today: Teach the Machine a Trick!

"Is there something you always do the same way? Like a sandwich — bread, butter, cheese?" Let the kid answer.

---

## Slide 3 — The Trick Card

"We can write such an order on a card, like a recipe. That's a Trick Card. The machine reads it only when you say the magic word."

---

## Slide 4 — Your Trick Card

"Here's the card. It has three lines: the trick's name, the magic word and three steps."

**[Grown-Up action]** Read the card aloud, don't fill it in yet.

---

## Slide 5 — Inventing the trick

"What shall we call the trick? What will the magic word be — one short word? And what are the three small steps?" Help make the steps small and clear.

**[Grown-Up action]** Write the kid's words on the card.

---

## Slide 6 — Giving the trick to the machine

"Now I'll put the card in a special folder. Then the machine will know this trick."

**[Grown-Up action]** Create `~/kid-ideas/.claude/skills/<word>/SKILL.md` from the card (see "For the Grown-Up").

---

## Slide 7 — Say the magic word!

"A new day begins — the machine remembers nothing. Say the magic word!" Let the kid say it aloud and you type it.

**[Grown-Up action]** Start a new session and run `/word`. Read aloud what the machine does.

---

## Slide 8 — Checking the steps

"Let's check against the card: was step one done? Step two? Step three?" Tick them off together.

---

## Slide 9 — If something's off

If something's off: "The machine didn't slip — our card wasn't quite exact. Let's make the step clearer."

**[Grown-Up action]** Fix the step in `SKILL.md` and run it again.

---

## Slide 10 — You're a trick teacher!

"Your card works for anyone! Shall we show it to a friend?" Ask: "How is a Trick Card different from an Idea Card?" "You're a trick teacher."
