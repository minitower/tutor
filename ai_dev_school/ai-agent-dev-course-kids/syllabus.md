# Syllabus

Each session: **For You (the kid) → Idea Card → For the Grown-Up → Show
& Tell**. Full detail lives in `modules/<session-name>/lesson.md` (English)
and `lesson.ru.md` (Russian), each paired with a simple on-screen slide deck
(`slides.md`/`slides.ru.md`) and a full facilitator script — the words the
supervising adult actually says out loud (`lecture.md`/`lecture.ru.md`). See
the table in [README.md](README.md) for direct links to every session's
files in both languages. Read [SUPERVISOR-GUIDE.md](SUPERVISOR-GUIDE.md)
before Session 0.

---

## Session 0 — Meet the Idea Machine
The machine can turn written words into a real thing you can see and
click. Make the simplest possible thing: a page with your name and your
favorite thing on it.
Idea Card: *"My name is ___. My favorite ___ is ___."*

## Session 1 — A Button That Does Something
Buttons can make things happen when you press them. Add one button to
your page that does something fun when pressed.
Idea Card: *"There's a button that says ___. When I press it, ___
happens."*

## Session 2 — Pick Your Own Colors
Make your page look like *yours* by choosing its colors on purpose,
instead of leaving it to chance.
Idea Card: pick colors from a small set of named swatches for the
background, the button, and the text.

## Session 3 — Say Exactly What You Mean
Try the same idea two ways — fuzzy, then exact — and see how much closer
the exact one gets to what you actually pictured.
Idea Card: two versions of the same idea, one loose ("a fun game"), one
exact ("a game where a cat catches three falling stars").

## Session 4 — What If...? A Story With Choices
Stories don't have to go just one way — build a tiny story where the
reader picks what happens next.
Idea Card: a short story start plus two or three choices, and what
happens for each one.

## Session 5 — Bug Hunt: Find What's Silly
Sometimes the machine gets something wrong or silly the first time.
Finding it and saying what's wrong is the fun part, not a failure.
Idea Card: intentionally ask for something with a small mistake in it,
find it together, then write what should happen instead.

## Session 6 — Show Someone
Show your thing to someone who's never seen it (a parent, sibling,
friend) and watch what they do — without helping them.
No new Idea Card — this session is about watching someone else use
something from Sessions 0–5.

## Session 7 — My Big Idea
Your own idea, start to finish — pick something you actually want to
make, fill out a full Idea Card covering what it does, what happens when
you press things, and what colors it uses, and build it.
Idea Card: the "big" version, combining everything from Sessions 0–4.

## Idea Card templates (for quick reference/printing)

**Basic thing (Session 0):**
> My name is ______. My favorite ______ is ______.

**Button (Session 1):**
> There's a button that says "______." When I press it, ______ happens.

**Colors (Session 2):**
> The background is ______. The button is ______. The words are
> ______.

**Exact vs. fuzzy (Session 3):**
> Fuzzy: "I want a fun ______."
> Exact: "I want a ______ where ______ [does what] to ______ [get
> what]."

**Story with choices (Session 4):**
> The story starts: ______.
> If you choose ______, then ______ happens.
> If you choose ______, then ______ happens.

**Big idea (Session 7):**
> What it's called: ______
> What happens when it starts: ______
> What happens when you press/choose ______: ______
> The colors are: ______
