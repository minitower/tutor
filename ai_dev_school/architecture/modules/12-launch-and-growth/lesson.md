# Module 12 — Launch & Growth: Getting Your First Users

## Learning objectives
- Understand how search engines and social media feeds decide what people see
- Make an app technically ready to be found and measured
- Plan a first launch with clear channels and metrics

## Lecture outline
1. How a search engine works: crawling → rendering → indexing → ranking; why SSR/SSG matters (link back to Module 2)
2. Technical SEO: `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, title/description, canonical, Open Graph, structured data (schema.org), Core Web Vitals, mobile-first
3. Google vs. Yandex: specifics, Google Search Console and Yandex Webmaster
4. Content SEO: search intent, keywords, landing pages per use case, links
5. How social media feeds rank: engagement signals (watch time, saves, shares, comments), early velocity, interest graph vs. social graph, why posting time and the first hour matter
6. Channels: Telegram, VK, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Habr, VC.ru, Product Hunt, Reddit, local communities; matching a channel to the audience
7. Positioning and the landing page: one audience, one problem, one call to action
8. Funnel and metrics: AARRR (acquisition, activation, retention, referral, revenue)
9. Analytics: Yandex Metrica, GA4, UTM tags, goals and events; privacy, cookie consent and personal data law basics

## Lab
Make "Slot" indexable (sitemap, meta, OG, structured data), submit it to webmaster tools, set up analytics with a "booking created" goal, write a launch plan for two channels.
- **With an agent:** ask the agent to audit the public page for technical SEO and to draft posts for each channel; rewrite the posts in your own voice.

## Deliverable
Passed SEO checklist + analytics dashboard with goals + launch plan (channels, content, dates, target metrics).
